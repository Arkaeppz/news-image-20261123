# The World in One Picture — Monday 23 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 23 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte.
`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a one-page broadsheet of that day's world news:
17 numbered stories with region and confidence tags, a day-in-numbers bar and
a date-check box, drawn by `build_image.py` from the day's reporting,
cross-checked across Principia's daily summary, the Roman Report, Broadcast
Prism, The Brief and News Media House:

- COP31, the longest UN climate summit, ends in Türkiye without a deal.
- Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya with sea drones; Kyiv confirms.
- Russian strikes kill at least 15 near Kyiv; a boy is pulled from rubble.
- Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; shares surge about 30%.
- A fire at an Indonesian sweets factory kills at least 24; ~30 missing.
- Trump warns of new strikes on Iran next year.
- Anthropic says its AI helped prove the Riemann hypothesis.
- BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury.
